# The lore of NEMO FRNS FARM, and how to play

## The story

A wild storm tossed your Rare Friend onto a tiny sandbar. It has no home and no food, only palms, driftwood and a
strange shrine humming in the sand.

Eight islanders already live here, and each one needs your help:

| Islander | Where | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Mayor Clam** | By the Bounty Board | Runs the island and the weekly Season. Knows everyone's business. |
| **Old Salt** | Fishing Dock | A fisherman who swears he's seen the Golden Nemo. |
| **Coral** | Market | The shopkeeper. Buys what you gather and sells seeds, bait and charms. |
| **Pip** | Near your home | The island kid. Full of tips for newcomers. |
| **Miner Moe** | Tide Mines | Boss of the tunnels, where greed is the real trap. |
| **Captain Rook** | Rival Harbor | Harbor master and friend to every raider with a plan. |
| **Smith Ember** | Volcano Forge | Says the forge only sings for the stubborn. |
| **Seer Anemone** | Kraken Reef | The reef oracle. Speaks in riddles about the Kraken and the deep. |

Talk to them every day. Each one has a daily gift, and what they say changes as your story moves on.

### Three acts

- **Act I: Washed Ashore (15 chapters).** Survive, build a hut, learn to trade, and unlock the island region by region,
  all the way to the Sea Castle.
- **Act II: Legends of the Deep (12 chapters).** Chase the Golden Nemo, forge a +10 legend, bring down the Kraken ten
  times, and find the Shrine's strangest gift: Rebirth.
- **Act III: Tides Eternal (never ends).** New chapters keep coming. Each one asks a little more and pays a little more.

### Your Friend's homeland

Every Rare Friend comes from one of nine family islands: Skeleton Isle, Mask Atoll, Family Cay, Cellular Reef,
Asymmetry Rock, Hoverer Heights, Colossus Crag, Sparkling Sands and Hollow Deep. From the **Voyage Pier** you can sail
home and search the ruins for **Family Relics**. Each relic gives +2% XP forever.

Your Friend's family also sets its **trait**, which is its play style:

| Family | Trait | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Skeleton | Bone Diver | +30% Abyss dive loot |
| Mask | Trickster | Raids steal 25% more and win 5% more often |
| Family | Homebody | Sleep restores 20% more, buildings cost 10% less SHELL |
| Cellular | Green Cells | Crops grow 25% faster, golden crops twice as likely |
| Asymmetry | Chaos Engine | Double or nothing wins 2% more often, Kraken crits hit harder |
| Hoverer | Glider | Moves 30% faster, wider fishing catch zone |
| Colossus | Titan | +25% raid and boss power, needs 15% more food |
| Sparkling | Sparkle Luck | +4% success on every Forge and Ascension roll |
| Hollow | Hollow Belly | Hunger drains 25% slower, mines reveal one safe tile |

Your Friend is drawn exactly as minted. It never gets recolored, and it earns a new hat and title every 10 levels
(Hatchling, Reef Scout, Tide Runner, Coral Knight, Abyss Walker and Ocean Legend), plus a star for every rebirth.

## How to play in two minutes

1. **Enter** with your wallet, or pick **Try as guest**.
2. **Move** by tapping or clicking anywhere, or with WASD and the arrow keys. Press **E** or the big orange button when you
   stand near something.
3. **Press Play.** The orange Play button lists every game and every place, and **Go** takes you there.
4. **Follow the note** under your needs bars. That's your current chapter. The gold arrow on the map points to where it
   happens, and **Go** takes you there. Tap the note to claim the reward.

### Staying alive

| Bar | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hunger (orange) | Drops over time. Below 25 you earn less and stop regaining energy. | **Tap the bar** or the flashing **Eat now** button to eat your best food. |
| Energy (yellow) | Every job and game costs energy. | Tap the bar to drink, or sleep at **Home**. |
| Mood (blue) | Above 75 means luck and bonus XP. Below 25 means bad luck. | Tap the bar for a treat. Sleeping in a big home helps too. |

You can get food by farming (kelp is ready in about 40 seconds), fishing at the Dock, buying bread at the Market or
cooking meals at Home.

### Getting rich (and risking it)

- **Safe money:** chop, quarry, farm, fish, fill bounties and sell at the Market.
- **Risky money:** the Abyss Dive, Tide Mines, double or nothing, raids, the Kraken and the Tide Wheel. Every game shows
  its odds before you play.
- **Voyages:** send your Friend away for 15 minutes, 1, 6 or 12 hours. Short trips are safe. The long ones usually come
  back empty but can return a hoard of RF, a Family Relic or simulated stock-token shares such as NVDA. While it's away,
  your Friend can't work.

### Getting stronger forever

- **Ascend** at the Coral Shrine to level up. It's a chance roll, and a failure never costs you a level.
- **Forge** your rod, blade and pick from +1 to +10 at the Volcano Forge.
- **Build** from a Beach Tent to a Sea Castle. Bigger homes rest you better and protect your vault from raids.
- **Unlock** 7 more regions of the island.
- **Rebirth** at level 30: start again at level 1 and keep your home, gear and items, with permanent bonuses.
- **Milestones** and the **daily streak** pay out on their own. Just play.
- **Season Journey:** a free 10-tier reward track on the Bounty Board that resets every week.

### Where your RF goes

Every RF you spend is split: 70% to the weekly Season Prize Pool, 25% to the creator, and 5% burned forever. The top 10
players each week share the pool. The full details are in [TOKENOMICS.md](TOKENOMICS.md). Everything is simulated.

### Playing with others

Everyone shares one island. You'll see other Friends walking and emoting, and you can raid them (they can raid you
back), fight the Kraken together and race up the same leaderboard. If nobody else is online, simulated rivals and
residents keep the island busy.
